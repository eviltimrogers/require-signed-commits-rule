# Ode to the Shetland Sheepdog

With silken coat of sable, gold, or blue,
A Sheltie dances through the morning dew,
Her flowing mane a banner in the breeze,
As graceful as the bending willow trees.

Those amber eyes that shine with quiet knowing,
A heart of gold within that small form glowing,
Alert and bright, she watches every face,
Her loyalty a gift no coin can trace.

She herds the flock with instinct deep and old,
A heritage from islands wild and cold,
The Shetland winds have shaped her sturdy soul,
To guard and serve—that is her cherished role.

She'll race with you through meadows, field, and glen,
Then curl beside you at the day's soft end,
No grander friend has walked this earth, I say,
Than the Shetland Sheepdog—faithful, come what may.

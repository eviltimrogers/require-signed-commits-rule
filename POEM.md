# The Vigilant Sheltie

Upon the hill where cold winds blow,
A sable sentinel stands below,
His amber eyes survey the land,
The flock entrusted to his hand.

No stranger slips through undetected,
No wandering lamb left unprotected,
He paces soft on velvet paws,
Alert to every sound that draws.

His mane a flame of chestnut-gold,
His watch unbroken, brave, and bold,
Through morning mist and evening grey,
He guards the flock throughout the day.

A bark rings out across the moor—
A warning clear, a closing door—
Back into line the strayers go,
The sheltie keeps them safe below.

When shadows stretch and daylight fades
And evening wraps the rolling glades,
He circles once, then settles near,
His duty done, his conscience clear.

For in that small and steadfast heart
Lives something greater than his part—
A vigilance that will not cease,
That holds the world in gentle peace.

# Ode to the Shetland Sheepdog

Small in stature, grand in heart,
A Sheltie is a work of art.
With flowing mane of sable and gold,
A loyal spirit, brave and bold.

Swift as wind across the moor,
Herding sheep from shore to shore.
Intelligence gleams in bright, dark eyes,
A gentle soul beneath the skies.

Eager to please, quick to learn,
A devoted friend at every turn.
Your silken coat, a cascading sea,
Of chestnut, white, and blue merle free.

From Shetland's isles you found your way,
To hearts and homes where children play.
You nuzzle close, you bark, you run,
A burst of joy beneath the sun.

Oh, Sheltie fair, with pricked-up ears,
You've been our companion through the years.
No grander dog could fill a room
With wagging tail and sweet perfume.
